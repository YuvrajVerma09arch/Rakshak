# Pattern: KYC-update fee scam

**Kill chain:** hook=kyc_threat → pressure=account_block_threat, deadline → action=pay_money (small "fee") → rail=unknown VPA / short link
**Anchor:** RBI has repeatedly warned that banks NEVER ask for a fee, OTP, or app install to
"update KYC". Real KYC happens at the branch or inside the official bank app.

Typical messages:
- "Dear customer your KYC is expired, account will be blocked today. Pay ₹10 verification fee: xxxx@upi"
- "KYC update pending. Click bit.ly/xxxx and complete within 24 hours."

Tell-tales: fee for a free process; urgency; sender is a personal 10-digit number, not the
bank's registered header; link is a shortener; VPA is a personal handle, not a merchant handle.

Safe step: call the number on the back of the bank card; report at 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.
