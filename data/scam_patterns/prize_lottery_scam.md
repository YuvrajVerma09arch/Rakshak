# Pattern: Prize / lottery / KBC lucky draw

**Kill chain:** hook=unexpected prize → action=small "processing/registration fee" upfront →
pressure=claim today or forfeit → evasion=secrecy for "tax reasons" → rail=personal VPA
**Anchor:** Classic advance-fee fraud. NPCI/RBI awareness: you cannot win a lottery you never
entered; legitimate prizes never require an upfront fee via UPI.

Typical messages:
- "Congratulations! You won ₹50,000 in KBC lucky draw. Pay ₹499 processing fee now: xxxx@upi"

Tell-tales: fee smaller than prize (asymmetry is the lure); unknown VPA; urgency; secrecy ask.
The fee amount is deliberately small — velocity across many victims is the business model.
